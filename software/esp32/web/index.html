Calibrate

Measure every LED with a luxmeter: dark room, meter always at the same spot and distance on the face. Tap a board: it lights red, green, blue and white in turn, one colour at a time at full power. Type each reading and press Enter; after white it moves on to the next board.

Home WiFi

Let the lamp join your WiFi, then open it from any device at home. Its own hotspot stays on as a fallback.

Firmware

Install a new firmware file (diskohedron.ino.bin). The lamp goes dark while it is written, then restarts, and this page reloads by itself. If anything goes wrong, the lamp keeps the current firmware.
